Dorm room tetris: a storage playbook

A standard dorm room gives you about 130 square feet, split with a roommate, and a strict no-damage policy that rules out shelves, hooks, and anything involving a drill. Everything below is tension-fit, adhesive, hanging, or freestanding — it survives a room inspection and a move-out in the same weekend. Ten picks, sorted by where they actually go in the room.

Split the room before you buy anything

Before ordering a single bin, walk the room with your roommate and assign zones — whose under-bed space is whose, which wall each desk backs onto. Most dorm storage failures aren't a product problem, they're a boundary problem: two people's stuff merging into one pile in the middle of the floor.
