The bathroom shelf you don't need a drill for

Rental bathrooms tend to give you one medicine cabinet and zero counter space, and the walls are almost always tiled — which means drilling isn't just against the lease, it can crack the tile and cost you the deposit outright. Every pick here stands on its own, wedges into place, or sticks with adhesive strips instead.

Work around the tile, not through it

Every pick above avoids the two things that get renters in trouble in a bathroom: drilling into tile and leaving adhesive residue behind. Test any adhesive strip on a small hidden patch of tile first if you're not sure how the finish will react.
