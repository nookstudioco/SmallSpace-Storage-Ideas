About this site

SmallSpace Storage Ideas is a library of storage guides for people living in small apartments, dorm rooms, and rentals — written for renters who can't drill into walls, can't build built-ins, and need every solution to survive a move.

Every guide is organized around two questions: does it actually fit, and can you take it with you when the lease ends. No product is featured just because it photographs well.
